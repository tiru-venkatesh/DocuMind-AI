Convert Image to Excel Instantly (Free AI Tool)

Img2XL is a free AI-powered tool that converts images, tables, receipts, and PDFs into structured Excel sheets within seconds.

How It Works

  1. Upload your image or PDF
  2. AI extracts tabular data
  3. Download as Excel file

PNG, JPG, JPEG, WEBP, scanned documents, and PDFs

Use Cases
